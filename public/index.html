How the voice channel works

The same agent brain answers on calls — the API is called with channel: "voice", so replies are short, spoken sentences. In this build the lead's speech is captured with the browser's speech recognition and the agent's voice is the browser's Hindi/Marathi voice. In production the call runs on Exotel/Twilio with Sarvam STT/TTS; the brain does not change.

What is real in this build

Every agent reply, classification, lead-profile update and tool call on this site is generated live by Gemini through /api/chat using your knowledge base. Nothing is scripted. Leads, conversations and analytics are stored in your browser (localStorage) for this preview.

What is simulated

Message delivery — you type as the lead instead of a real WhatsApp number, and calls use the browser's microphone and voices. Tool calls are logged and update the CRM but do not yet hit Razorpay/Zoom/Exotel.

Responsible AI

Discloses it is an AI when asked. No income guarantees, no fake scarcity, no invented facts (knowledge-base grounding). Refunds, complaints and founder requests are escalated to a human. DPDP-compliant consent at the lead form in production.
